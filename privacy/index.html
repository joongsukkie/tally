Tally

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026.

Tally doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your entries

Everything you log (amounts, currencies, categories, notes and dates) is stored only on your iPhone. It's included in your regular iCloud or computer backup of the device, the same as any app's data, but the developer never receives it and has no way to see it.

Exchange rates

To convert foreign-currency expenses, Tally downloads public daily exchange-rate tables from the open currency-api dataset, served by jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) with a Cloudflare Pages mirror (currency-api.pages.dev). Each request asks only for the table for a particular date. It contains no identifier, no amounts and nothing you've typed. As with any web request, those networks see your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies (jsDelivr, Cloudflare).

Exports

If you use "Export as CSV", the file goes wherever you choose in the share sheet. Tally doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Erase all entries inside Tally, or delete the app. Either removes everything from your iPhone.

Children

Tally collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
