Support

Something not working? Open an issue and describe what happened. Please don't include personal financial details.

How do I log something for an earlier day?

Tap the day on the calendar, then + Add on its receipt. Or change the date in the entry sheet's When row.

How do I edit or delete an entry?

Tap the entry to open it. Change anything and tap Save, or tap Delete entry at the bottom. You can also long-press an entry for Edit and Delete.

Which exchange rate does it use?

The daily reference rate for the day of the expense. For today and yesterday it uses the latest published rate. If you know exactly what your card was charged, tap Exact charge and type it in; Tally keeps that number instead.

What does the ≈ next to an amount mean?

You logged it while offline, so Tally used the last rate it had saved. Next time your iPhone is online it swaps in the proper rate for that day, automatically.

I changed my home currency. What happened to my entries?

Every entry, and your budget, was converted to the new currency at the rate for its own day. The amount you actually paid in the original currency is always kept, so nothing is lost.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Your entries are part of your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup brings them back. For a copy you can open in a spreadsheet, use Settings → Export as CSV.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads.
