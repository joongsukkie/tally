Tally

A running tab of what you spend.

Tally is an iPhone expense diary printed like a receipt. The month so far sits at the top, every day is on a calendar, and the charts show where it all went.

What it does

Log an expense with a big keypad and one of six categories: food, drinks, shopping, entertainment, transportation, other. Pay in any currency and it's converted at that day's exchange rate, or at the exact amount your card charged. Pie charts with percentages and amounts, trend bars for any week, month or year, and an optional monthly budget.

What it doesn't do

No account, no ads, no tracking, no subscription. Your entries never leave your iPhone.
